Sine Wave Physics: Spatial vs. Temporal Representation

Interactive visualization of sinusoidal wave equations: \( y(x, t) = A \sin\left(2\pi \left(\frac{x}{\lambda} - \frac{t}{T}\right)\right) \)

1. Spatial Wave Profile: \( y \) vs. Position \( x \)

Snapshot of the wave across space at fixed time \( t = t_0 \). Distance between adjacent crests is Wavelength (\( \lambda \)).

2. Temporal Wave Profile: \( y \) vs. Time \( t \)

Oscillation history of a single medium particle over time at fixed position \( x = x_0 = 0 \). Duration of one cycle is Period (\( T \)).

Key Physics Formulas

Wave Speed (\(v\)):
v = λ / T = f • λ = 2.00 m/s
Frequency (\(f\)):
f = 1 / T = 0.50 Hz
Wavenumber (\(k\)):
k = 2π / λ = 1.57 rad/m
Angular Frequency (\(ω\)):
ω = 2π / T = 3.14 rad/s
